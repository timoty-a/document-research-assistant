Review the states

The separate preview bar on every screen switches between populated, empty, loading, uploading, success and two error views. Upload and Refresh only change the preview. Choose a file selects a synthetic filename; it never opens or reads your files.

Open Details to inspect a record’s full ID and exact byte size. The two interview transcripts intentionally share a name. In D, inspect initial loading, refresh with existing rows, a failed refresh, definite rejection and an uncertain save. Its successful upload adds methods-summary.txt and increases the populated library from five to six records.
